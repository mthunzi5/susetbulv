Our Story

Since December, we've been building something special.

Sunset BLVD Cocktail Lounge is Umhlanga's home for slow mornings, long lunches and golden-hour cocktails. From sunrise breakfast plates to signature drinks crafted with intention, every detail is designed around good food, great company, and a perfect setting.
